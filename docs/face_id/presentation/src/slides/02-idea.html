The idea

Who’s there? The board answers by itself.

The app recognises up to four enrolled people with the board’s own camera and chip. No cloud, no laptop, no internet connection.

The board’s real screen with the detected face, its five landmarks and the result; captured with build faceid-14, before the recognizer switch (slide 15).
How it works
1
Enrol
Tap ENROLL and turn your head a little: the board records six samples of your face.
2
Recognise
Look at the camera: in most cases the name appears at the first look. A stranger gets UNKNOWN.
3
Remember
Up to four people, kept in the board’s flash memory through restarts and updates.
